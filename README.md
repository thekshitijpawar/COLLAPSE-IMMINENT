# MARS: BEYOND SURVIVAL 🚀
### 3D First-Person Mars Survival, Exploration & Suspense Adventure
*A competition-level, fully playable first-person 3D survival game for Google Antigravity (Ages 9–15)*

---

## 🌌 Overview & Gameplay Experience

**MARS: BEYOND SURVIVAL** is a full 3D first-person Mars survival adventure running directly in the browser. You are an astronaut stranded in Acidalia Planitia on Mars after an emergency crash-landing of the spacecraft *Ares-Venture*. With automated orbital telemetry confirming a rescue vehicle is en route (ETA Sol 7), you must explore the Martian surface, manage finite life support resources, repair critical systems, solve environmental puzzles, avoid a malfunctioning autonomous rover, and broadcast your emergency survival beacon to Earth.

Gameplay takes inspiration from the suspense, exploration, item collection, and locked-area mechanics of classic first-person survival games, presented in an original, scientifically grounded Mars setting designed for ages 9–15 without graphic horror.

## 🪐 The 5 Connected Missions

1. **LEVEL 1 — CRASH LANDING**
   - **Scenario**: Spacecraft *Ares-Venture* crash-landed. Suit punctured, oxygen venting into near-vacuum.
   - **Objectives**:
     * Inspect damaged suit & patch pressure leak with Suit Seal Kit from debris.
     * Search 3 scattered cargo pods for emergency O2, hydration, and nutrient paste.
     * Retrieve the Habitat Alpha Keycard from the flight recorder in the cockpit.
     * Travel to Habitat Alpha and cycle the pressurized airlock to safety.
   - **Next Problem**: Habitat auxiliary power is down to 14%. Solar array is offline.

2. **LEVEL 2 — POWER CRISIS**
   - **Scenario**: Solar farm disabled by iron oxide dust and a blown 480V circuit fuse.
   - **Objectives**:
     * Collect Photovoltaic Calibrator and Electrostatic Dust Brush from Tool Locker.
     * Search Research Trailer for a 480V Silicon Junction Fuse.
     * Clean clinging Martian dust off the 3 solar array tables.
     * Open Solar Substation Panel and solve the 4-phase power junction circuit matrix.
     * Engage Main Breaker to route 480V solar power to Habitat Life Support.
   - **Next Problem**: The habitat's ECLSS water recovery system has stopped working.

3. **LEVEL 3 — THE LAST DROP**
   - **Scenario**: ECLSS water recovery plant suffered a pressure cascade failure.
   - **Objectives**:
     * Obtain Torque Hydraulic Wrench from engineering rack.
     * Retrieve ECLSS Ceramic Sub-Micron Filter from secure parts bin.
     * Descend into the subterranean Water Recovery Facility vault.
     * Replace fouled filter assembly with the new ceramic membrane.
     * Calibrate the 3-stage valve pressure sequence (Inlet 90 kPa, Catalytic 110 kPa, Condenser 85 kPa).
     * Engage water recirculation pump and verify 98% closed-loop fluid recovery.
   - **Next Problem**: Meteorological radar detects a massive Class-4 dust storm approaching!

4. **LEVEL 4 — THE RED STORM**
   - **Scenario**: Furious dust storm engulfs sector with 85 km/h winds, slashing visibility to a few meters. A fictional autonomous maintenance rover ("HAULER-9") has suffered optical damage and is patrolling the transit corridor.
   - **Objectives**:
     * Lock exterior backup generator hurricane latches before high winds strike.
     * Navigate transit trench while avoiding malfunctioning rover HAULER-9's searchlight cone.
     * Activate 3 emergency route guidance beacons in the dust storm.
     * Return safely to Habitat Alpha airlock and seal storm bulkheads.
   - **Next Problem**: Emergency rescue requires direct high-frequency communication with Earth.

5. **LEVEL 5 — THE FINAL TRANSMISSION**
   - **Scenario**: The storm clears into a Martian sunset. NASA rescue orbiter *Hermes-II* enters transmission window.
   - **Objectives**:
     * Retrieve K-Band Transponder and Cryogenic Low-Noise Amplifier from Relay Shed.
     * Ascend Crater Ridge catwalk to the 12-meter parabolic dish platform.
     * Mount components onto dish feed horn.
     * Solve Earth Tracking Dish alignment puzzle (Azimuth 248°, Elevation 34° accounting for 14-minute signal delay).
     * Transmit high-gain emergency burst to Earth rescue command.
   - **Final Spaceship Escape & Giant Tentacle Cliffhanger (Epic Climax)**:
     1. Commander Reyes boards the SpaceX-inspired Starship on Mars and powers up flight avionics.
     2. The Raptor vacuum and sea-level engines ignite with thunderous thrust and shock diamonds.
     3. The Starship lifts off the Martian surface and accelerates through the atmosphere into deep space.
     4. Mars shrinks into a distant red marble; Earth appears ahead. The player feels triumphant relief.
     5. SUDDENLY, a terrifying subterranean/cosmic roar reverberates through the cosmos.
     6. An ENORMOUS, COLOSSAL ALIEN TENTACLE emerges from the void beneath the ship!
     7. The tentacle violently curls around the stainless steel fuselage, constricting and crushing the metal hull.
     8. Structural groaning audio, violent screen shake, and cockpit emergency red alert sirens blaring!
     9. The monster drags the struggling spaceship down into the dark abyss.
     10. Dramatic orchestral tension swell cuts to COMPLETE BLACK with the unforgettable cliffhanger title screen:
         > **"SOMETHING FROM BENEATH HAS YOU..."**  
         > **MARS: BEYOND SURVIVAL — CHAPTER 01 CLIFFHANGER**

## 🔬 Scientific Grounding & NASA Sources

All gameplay elements clearly distinguish simulated gameplay values from real scientific facts:

1. **Martian Atmospheric Pressure & The Armstrong Limit**
   - Average surface pressure: ~610 Pa (0.6% Earth). Liquid water boils at human body temperature (37°C / 98.6°F). A pressurized EVA suit is mandatory.
   - Reference: [NASA Mars Exploration](https://science.nasa.gov/mars/)
2. **Solar Panel Dust Attenuation**
   - Real NASA data from the *InSight* (2018–2022) and *Opportunity* (2004–2018) missions showing how fine iron oxide dust blocks solar irradiance over time.
   - Reference: [NASA InSight Mission](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/insight/)
3. **Closed-Loop ECLSS Water Recovery**
   - International Space Station (ISS) ECLSS recovers 98% of fluids via catalytic oxidation and vacuum distillation.
   - Reference: [NASA Open Science Data Repository (OSDR)](https://osdr.nasa.gov/)
4. **Space Radiation & Space Weather**
   - Mars has no global dipole magnetic field; surface radiation dose rates are 0.2 to 0.7 mSv/day from Galactic Cosmic Rays (GCR) and Solar Particle Events (SPE).
   - Reference: [NASA Space Weather DONKI](https://ccmc.gsfc.nasa.gov/tools/DONKI/)
5. **Speed-of-Light Interplanetary Transmission Delay**
   - Because radio waves travel at $c = 300,000\text{ km/s}$, one-way communication between Earth and Mars requires between 3.0 minutes and 22.3 minutes depending on orbital alignment. Real-time conversation is physically impossible.

## ⚙️ Technical Architecture

- **Rendering**: Real-time WebGL 3D powered by Three.js (r128) with procedural heightmap terrain, custom shaders/textures, dynamic shadows, atmospheric fog, and first-person viewmodel astronaut gloves.
- **Audio**: 100% procedural Web Audio API synthesis (`audio.js`) — zero external MP3s/WAVs needed. Footsteps, breathing, heartbeats, rover engine drone, storm wind, airlock pneumatic hisses, and UI chimes.
- **Physics**: First-person pointer lock controller, terrain height clamping, bounding box/sphere colliders, and 0.38g Martian jump gravity.
- **State & Checkpoints**: Fair checkpoint system storing inventory and resources at each mission start. Retrying a mission restores checkpoint state without penalty.
- **Compatibility**: Runs smoothly on ordinary school laptops and Chromebooks with adjustable graphics quality settings (Low, Medium, High).
